Check it yourself

Models make mistakes. Look closely at these spots:

  • Eyes and reflections: do the light reflections in both eyes match?
  • Ears and earrings: are the ears the same shape, and do the earrings match?
  • Hair edges: does the hair blur or melt into the background?
  • Teeth: are they a strange number, or merged into one strip?
  • Background warping: do straight lines bend near the face?
  • Lighting: do the shadows on the face match the light in the scene?
